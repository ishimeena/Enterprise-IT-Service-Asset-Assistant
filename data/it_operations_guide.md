## Laptop Replacement Policy

- Request a replacement through the IT service desk when a laptop is damaged, unreliable, or no longer meets role requirements.
- Include the asset tag, a description of the problem, and any troubleshooting already completed.
- IT will assess the device, confirm approval, and arrange a replacement when appropriate.
- Return the original device and accessories as directed. Do not dispose of or transfer company equipment yourself.
- Back up work to approved company storage before returning a device. IT will advise on data transfer and secure wiping.

## Incident Priority Guide

- **High:** A critical business service is unavailable, multiple people are affected, or there is a suspected security incident. Contact the service desk promptly and describe the impact.
- **Medium:** A user or team is blocked from an important task, but a workaround or alternative is available.
- **Low:** The issue has limited impact, does not block work, or is a routine request.
- Select priority based on business impact and urgency, not personal preference. Provide affected users, services, and relevant deadlines when submitting a ticket.
- Report suspected loss of data, unauthorized access, or malware immediately through the designated security reporting channel.

## VPN Troubleshooting

1. Confirm that your internet connection is working.
2. Verify that you are using the company-approved VPN client and your current account credentials.
3. Disconnect and reconnect once, then restart the VPN client if the issue continues.
4. Note the error message, time of failure, device asset tag, and whether other network services are affected.
5. If you still cannot connect, open a ticket with Network Support. Never send your password or authentication codes in a ticket.

## Lost or Stolen Device Procedure

- Report a lost or stolen company device to the service desk and your manager as soon as possible.
- If theft is suspected, follow local procedures for contacting the appropriate authorities.
- Provide the device asset tag, last known location and time, and a reliable way to contact you.
- Do not attempt to recover the device yourself or remove company security controls.
- IT will assess the incident and take appropriate steps to protect company accounts and data. Cooperate with any follow-up requests.

## Software Installation Rules

- Install only software approved and provided through company IT channels.
- Submit a service request for new software, licenses, or elevated permissions, including the business need.
- Do not use personal accounts, unapproved downloads, or license keys to install software on company devices.
- Do not bypass security controls or install updates from unofficial sources.
- Contact IT if an application is needed urgently or an approved installation fails.

## Ticket Escalation Process

- Add relevant details and updates to the existing ticket rather than creating duplicate tickets.
- If the issue worsens, affects additional users, or prevents critical work, update the ticket with the changed impact and urgency.
- Contact the service desk if the issue requires prompt attention or the ticket is blocked pending information.
- The service desk may assign the ticket to another support team or escalate it based on impact, urgency, and specialist needs.
- For suspected security incidents, use the designated security reporting channel immediately instead of waiting for a routine ticket update.
